Message from Masashi Kishimoto

Thank you for the incredible amount of votes!
It was fun seeing how results varied from region to region.
I was shocked to see Minato at the top for all regions, and the fact that Sakura is so beloved by many people around the world made me proud as a writer.
To tell you the truth, I was actually rooting for Kurama.
I was planning on drawing only the top 20 characters, but I really wanted to add Kurama, who was close at 22nd...
So I ended up including him and Guy, who was in 21st place!
Sorry for completely ignoring the original plan to have the top 20 characters for the 20th anniversary because of my personal attachment.
Right now I'm working on the short manga for Minato who placed 1st.
While trying to make a story that would match the expectations of his fans, I ended up with more pages than I had originally anticipated.
Please look forward to the secret story behind Minato's jutsu!
I want to thank you all very much again for voting!

Ranking by Age

These rankings could be made thanks to the gender and age group information submitted by our participants upon voting. An astounding percentage of the total votes were from fans in their 20s, who cast over 60%!
The overall first place winner, Minato Namikaze, was also the clear leader in this age group.
However, in the 40-49 age group, Kakashi Hatake received the highest amount of votes!
We can clearly feel the love our adult NARUTO fans have for Kakashi.

Comeback Ranking
*Calculated from popularity gained after the Midterm Report.

We ranked the characters whose positions improved between the Midterm Report and the end of the voting period. The character whose rank increased the most was Yamato, who jumped up a whole nine places! Gengetsu Hozuki was at 100th and rose six places, allowing him to enter the "NARUTOP99" final ranking. Other characters with limited appearances also managed to leave a strong enough impression to win a spot in the final ranking.

Perfect Attendance Ranking
*Calculation of participants who voted for a character every day.

The voting period started on December 18th, 2022 (JST) and ended on January 31st, 2023 (JST). We created this ranking for the many users who made sure to show up and vote for the same character every day! Please note that we've limited this ranking to the characters who were unfortunately unable to break into the top 20. In first place we have Iruka Umino, who is very popular in Japan! In second place we have Tenten, followed by Orochimaru in third. To the fans who voted, thank you for your love and support. You all get perfect attendance awards.

REGION’S SPOTLIGHT
*Highlights characters that gained a markedly greater number of votes in the specified region of the world.

For the REGION'S SPOTLIGHT of the final results, we've selected characters who placed in the top 99 globally, but gained a markedly greater number of votes in the specified region of the world. In Japan, the birthplace of NARUTO, Kakashi Hatake, Naruto Uzumaki, and Sasuke Uchiha made the ranking. In Oceania, Sakumo Hatake was popular, while in Europe, Shisui Uchiha got a lot of love.
